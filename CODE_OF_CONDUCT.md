# AstraBridge Code of Conduct

## Status and Scope

This policy applies to AstraBridge project spaces, including future issue,
review, discussion, and contribution channels. It is effective as a conduct
standard now; broad public contributor intake remains gated on publishing a
private enforcement contact as described below.

## Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:

- be respectful, constructive, and specific about technical disagreement;
- welcome good-faith questions from developers with different backgrounds;
- focus criticism on behavior, evidence, interfaces, and proposed changes;
- protect other people's privacy, credentials, and unpublished work; and
- acknowledge uncertainty, failures, and reduced-authority behavior honestly.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory or hateful
language, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, publishing
credentials or private data, knowingly misleading users about safety or model
authority, and sustained disruption of a technical collaboration space.

## Enforcement Principles

Maintainers should assess reports using the available evidence and the safety
of affected people. Responses can range from clarification or a warning to
removal of content, temporary participation limits, or a permanent ban from
project spaces. A maintainer with a direct conflict should not be the sole
reviewer of that report.

## Reporting and Privacy

No private conduct-reporting contact has been configured yet. Before AstraBridge
opens broad public contributor intake, the maintainer must publish a monitored
private contact and a response expectation in this file or its linked policy.

Until that happens, do not post sensitive personal details in a public issue,
pull request, or discussion. This limitation is intentional and is a
developer-preview release gate, not a request to use an insecure channel.
